## Autonomous recheck after the audit

A five-business recheck used the original license and activity source, then searched the web again. It found the broken website link for Action Paint, published a reviewed preview, routed the draft to phone follow-up and skipped Nice Day after verifying its contact site. Pine Tree, Zunigas and Luxury stayed on hold during this recheck. Luxury’s homepage delivered a JavaScript shell to SiteScout’s HTML reader. A human could corroborate its residential subdomain using another source. The research trace preserves that difference.
